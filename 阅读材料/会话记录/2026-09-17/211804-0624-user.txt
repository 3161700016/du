style="box-sizing: border-box;">1.protection

n..保护

音标：[prəˈtekʃn]

英英释义：Protection is the act of protecting someone or something from being harmed, damaged or attacked.

词根词源助记：pro-（在前）+ tegere（遮盖），“提前遮住”即保护；同根词 detect（揭开→察觉）。

style="box-sizing: border-box;">2.flagship

n..旗舰；王牌

音标：[ˈflæɡʃɪp]

英英释义：A flagship is the most important ship in a fleet of ships, especially the one on which the commander of the fleet is sailing.

词根词源助记：flag（旗）+ ship（船），舰队中司令官所在的指挥舰，引申为同类中最重要的一个。

style="box-sizing: border-box;">3.downgrade

v..使降级；下调

音标：[ˌdaʊnˈɡreɪd]

英英释义：If someone or something is downgraded, they are given a lower rank or position than before.

例句：The species was downgraded from "endangered" to "vulnerable".

style="box-sizing: border-box;">4.vulnerable

adj..易受伤害的

音标：[ˈvʌlnərəbl]

英英释义：A person, animal or place that is vulnerable is easily hurt or harmed physically, mentally or emotionally.

辨析：近义 at risk / exposed，反义 invulnerable；辨析：endangered 强调“种群濒临灭绝”，vulnerable 只说“易受伤害”——从 endangered 降为 vulnerable，程度减轻。

style="box-sizing: border-box;">5.testimony

n..明证；见证

音标：[ˈtestɪməni]

英英释义：If one thing is a testimony to another, it shows clearly that the other thing exists or is true.

词根词源助记：拉丁语 testis（证人），同根词 testify（作证）、attest（证明）。

style="box-sizing: border-box;">6.undertake

v..承担；着手做

音标：[ˌʌndəˈteɪk]

英英释义：When you undertake a task or job, you start doing it and accept responsibility for it.

例句：Over the years, China has undertaken a raft of measures to protect wild animals.

style="box-sizing: border-box;">7.habitat

n..栖息地

音标：[ˈhæbɪtæt]

英英释义：The habitat of an animal or plant is the natural environment in which it normally lives or grows.

词根词源助记：拉丁语 habitare（居住），同根词 inhabit（居住于）。

style="box-sizing: border-box;">8.recognition

n..认可；赞誉

音标：[ˌrekəɡˈnɪʃn]

英英释义：If something is given recognition, people accept that it is important or valuable.

词根词源助记：re-（再）+ gnoscere（知道），同根词 cognition（认知）、diagnose（诊断）。

style="box-sizing: border-box;">9.strengthen

v..加强

音标：[ˈstreŋθn]

英英释义：If you strengthen something, you make it stronger or more effective.

例句：Continued efforts will be made to strengthen the protection of giant pandas.
