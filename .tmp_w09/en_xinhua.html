Beijing Winter Olympics venues draw interest from the public

Source: Xinhua

Editor: huaxia

2023-02-03 18:26:17

BEIJING, Feb. 3, 2023 (Xinhua) -- Driven by the 2022 Beijing Winter Olympics, ice and snow tourism has gradually gained popularity among Chinese people. Various competition venues, as the legacy of the Beijing Winter Olympics, offered easy access to ice-and-snow facilities for the general public.
